Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about LessonAI.

Is LessonAI really 100% free?
Yes! LessonAI is completely free to use. There are no hidden charges, no credit card requirements, and no sign-up needed. We believe in providing free tools for educators worldwide.
Do I need to create an account to generate a lesson plan?
No account is required. Simply enter your subject, grade level, and topic on our tool page, and click "Generate." It's that simple.
Can I edit the generated lesson plan?
Absolutely. The AI generates a highly structured draft. You can easily copy the text, save it as a PDF, or print it and make your own custom edits to fit your specific classroom needs.
Does it support different curriculums (IB, Common Core, etc.)?
Yes. Our advanced tool allows you to select your specific curriculum framework and instruction language, ensuring the plan aligns with your regional educational standards.
How accurate are the lesson plans?
Our AI is trained on vast educational data, but it can occasionally make mistakes. We always recommend that teachers review and verify the content before presenting it in a classroom setting.
Can I use LessonAI on my mobile phone?
Yes, our website is fully responsive and optimized for mobile devices, tablets, and desktop computers.
